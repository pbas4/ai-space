# RW CRM review checklist

Plan review: context is fresh and sufficient, scope and interfaces are bounded, verification is explicit, and UI-library-over-Figma decisions are recorded.

Implementation review: confirm behavior, accessibility, tests, and every relevant UI-library decision. Critical findings block completion; other findings are reported.

Review plans and implementations for context completeness, UI-library authority, Figma conflicts, component boundaries, accessibility, verification coverage, and approval readiness. Critical findings block completion; lower-severity findings are reported.
